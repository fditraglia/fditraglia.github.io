# University of Oxford

- [Empirical Research Methods](https://ditraglia.com/erm) (Graduate): a flipped-classroom course on applied econometrics, R programming, and reproducible research, with video lectures, slides, and problem sets. A draft companion book is available at [empirical-methods.com](https://empirical-methods.com).
- [Treatment Effects](https://treatment-effects.com) (Graduate): lecture notes and short video lectures on causal inference, along with the slides and R practicals for my two short courses, *Treatment Effects: The Basics* and *Treatment Effects: Beyond the Basics*.
- [Limited Dependent Variables](https://economictricks.com) (Graduate): lecture notes, slides, video lectures, and problem sets with solutions from my part of the first-year MPhil econometrics course.
- [Introduction to Probability and Statistics](/prelims-prob-stats) (Undergraduate Tutorials): syllabus, review questions, and flashcard decks.
- [Quantitative Economics](/QE) (Undergraduate Tutorials): tutorial arrangements, with links to related posts on my blog.
- [Behavioral and Experimental Economics](BEE.html) (Undergraduate Tutorials): tutorial schedule and guidance for the group research project.
- [Econometrics](/pdf/econometrics-tutorials.pdf) (Undergraduate Tutorials): tutorial assignments.
